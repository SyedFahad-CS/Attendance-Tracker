# 📚 MCA Attendance Tracker — Autumn 2025

A modern, mobile-friendly web app to track your semester attendance, plan your classes, and stay above the threshold. Designed for MCA 1st Semester, but easily adaptable for any course or university!

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Interactive Timetable:**  
  Tap cells to mark Present, Absent, or Clear.  
  See your attendance at a glance.

- **Per-Course Analytics:**  
  Visual cards show attendance percentage and stats for each subject.

- **Attendance Planning:**  
  See how many classes you need to attend (or can skip) to stay above the threshold.

- **Semester Settings:**  
  Set your semester end date and holidays.  
  Remaining classes are calculated automatically.

- **Mobile Responsive:**  
  Works beautifully on Android, iOS, tablets, and desktops.

- **Export & Import:**  
  Backup your attendance data or move it to another device.

- **Print-Friendly Reports:**  
  Print your attendance summary for submission or records.

- **Course Filtering:**  
  Focus on a single course or view all at once.

- **Customizable Threshold:**  
  Adjust the attendance threshold to match your university’s requirements.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Clone or Download:**  
   Download the project files or clone the repo.

2. **Open in Browser:**  
   Open `index.html` in Chrome, Firefox, or any modern browser.

3. **Mark Attendance:**  
   Tap timetable cells to mark your attendance.

4. **Set Semester Details:**  
   Enter your semester end date and holidays in the settings section.

5. **Export/Import Data:**  
   Use the Export/Import buttons to backup or restore your attendance.

---

## 🛠️ Customization

- **Edit Courses & Timetable:**  
  Update the `COURSES` and `TIMETABLE_DATA` objects in `index.html` to match your subjects and schedule.

- **Change Threshold:**  
  Use the slider at the top to set your attendance requirement.
